yingyang backup

A private, single-user backup tool.

yingyang backup is a personal utility that copies files from the owner's own computer to the owner's own Google Drive account. It exists so that one person's work is not lost when a machine dies. There are no other users, no sign-up, and no service to join.

What it does

On a schedule, it compares a local folder with a folder in Google Drive and uploads whatever changed. Files are encrypted before they leave the machine. Nothing is ever read back out of Drive except the backup itself, on the day a restore is needed.

What it does not do

It does not look at, index, analyse or share anything else in the Drive account. It does not collect analytics, and it does not talk to any server other than Google's.
