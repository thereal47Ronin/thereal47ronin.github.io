Privacy policy

yingyang backup — last updated 19 November 2026

This policy covers the application yingyang backup ("the app"), a private backup utility used by a single person on their own computer. The app is not offered to the public and has exactly one user: its owner.

1. What Google account data the app accesses

With the user's explicit permission, the app requests the Google Drive scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive). It uses that access to:

  • create a folder in the user's own Google Drive;
  • list the contents of that folder;
  • upload, update and delete the backup files that the app itself placed there.

The app does not read, download, index, modify or delete any other file in the account. It accesses no other Google service, and it does not request the user's name, email, contacts, calendar or location.

2. Where the data goes

Files travel directly between the user's computer and Google Drive over HTTPS. There is no intermediary server, no relay and no third party. Backups are encrypted on the user's machine before upload, so the stored copies are unreadable without a key that never leaves that machine.

3. Storage and retention

The only copy of the data outside the user's computer is in the user's own Google Drive account, under the user's own Google terms. The app keeps no copy anywhere else. Deleting the backup folder in Drive, or revoking the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, removes the app's access entirely.

4. Sharing

Nothing is shared, sold, published or transferred to anyone. No data is used for advertising, profiling, analytics or training. No third-party service receives any of it.

5. Security

Access is granted through Google's OAuth 2.0 flow and can be revoked by the user at any time. Credentials are stored locally on the user's machine only. Backup contents are encrypted before they leave the machine.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children and has no users other than its owner.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will replace this page with a new date at the top.
